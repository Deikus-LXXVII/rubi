Rubi GitHub

Your agent does the work.
You keep the keys.

Rubi lets your AI agent use your accounts, starting with iCloud Mail. Everything is encrypted with a key only you hold, and anything sensitive waits for your Face ID.

Tell your Grok Bot:

Install Rubi-Project: run curl -fsSL https://rubi-panel.com/install.sh | sh

How it works

  1. 1

    Install from the chat

    Your agent installs Rubi on its own computer and sends you a link.

  2. 2

    Set up Face ID

    Rubi is locked with a passkey on your phone. Your agent never sees your key or your passwords.

  3. 3

    Approve what matters

    Reading and drafts can run freely. Sending waits for you: the panel shows the exact email, and your Face ID approves it.

Your key, not the agent's

Rubi starts locked and opens only with your passkey or password. Nobody can recover your key.

Approvals you can trust

Face ID signs the exact action. The agent can't approve, change the email after you see it, or change settings.

No servers of ours

Rubi runs on your agent's machine. This page is static and stores nothing.

Open and verifiable

MIT-licensed, signed releases with build provenance, and a binary that checks itself.

Plugin store

Rubi starts bare. Add integrations as plugins: each one is signed, reviewed, and shows what it can do before you approve it.
